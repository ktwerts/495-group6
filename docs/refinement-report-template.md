# Individual Refinement Report (exactly 250 words)

Each team member writes their own report. Use this outline to hit every grading criterion, then
remove the headings and guidance and check that the word count is exactly 250.

**Name / Role / Module reviewed by:**

1. **Review process (~40 words).** Who reviewed your module, how (GitHub PR review on PR #__),
   and which checklist areas they covered: quality, security, performance, integration and
   documentation.

2. **Feedback received and changes made (~80 words).** Give two or three specific findings.
   For each, say what the reviewer found, what you changed, and the commit or PR reference.
   For example: "Reviewer noted the shift list ran one query per employee; I replaced it
   with a single join (commit abc123)."

3. **Technical debt identified (~70 words).** Name one or two concrete instances from
   [technical-debt.md](technical-debt.md) by ID, such as TD-1, no login rate limiting.
   Explain the shortcut, why the team took it (deadline or scope), and its "interest": the
   maintenance, reliability or security cost.

4. **Mitigation strategy (~45 words).** Give actionable steps and when they'll happen (before
   Beta or later). Balance delivery speed against long-term quality.

5. **Reflection (~15 words).** Write a professional, objective statement on what you'd do
   differently.

Count the words before you submit (in Word or Google Docs, use the word count tool).
